What is the difference?

Both techniques turn a 3D scene into a 2D image, but they work in opposite directions.

Rasterization

Three.js · WebGL · games

How
Projects every triangle onto the screen and fills the pixels it covers.
Speed
Dozens of frames per second, even on a phone.
Realism
Reflections, shadows and glass are approximations, built with environment maps, shadow maps and shader tricks.
Limit
Objects cannot "see" each other: the metal sphere does not reflect the small balls around it.

Ray tracing

C++17 · this project

How
Shoots rays from the camera and follows the light as it bounces around the scene.
Speed
Seconds or minutes per image.
Realism
Reflections, refraction and soft shadows emerge on their own from the physics, no tricks.
Bonus
Colored light spills from one object onto another (global illumination).

One scene, two versions
